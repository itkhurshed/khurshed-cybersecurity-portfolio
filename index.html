I'm a Cybersecurity Specialist and System Administrator with over 15 years of experience keeping enterprise environments online, monitored, and defensible — across financial services, energy, and technology sectors. My day-to-day centers on SOC-style operations: SIEM monitoring, log analysis, threat detection, and incident response, backed by deep infrastructure expertise in Microsoft 365, Azure, Active Directory, and enterprise networking.

I hold CompTIA CySA+, Microsoft Certified: Azure Administrator Associate, and Cisco CCNP Enterprise / CCNA credentials. I care about building infrastructure that doesn't just work — it holds up under pressure, scales cleanly, and stays defensible against real-world threats.

  • SIEM monitoring, threat detection & incident response
  • Log analysis & vulnerability management
  • Windows Server, Active Directory & Group Policy management
  • Network design, routing/switching & VPN infrastructure (CCNP/CCNA)
  • Enterprise Microsoft 365, Azure AD & Intune administration
